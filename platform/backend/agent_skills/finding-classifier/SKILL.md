---
name: finding-classifier
description: Classify SC onsite audit notes as C, OFI, NC-Minor, NC-Major or Needs Review.
---

Assess each control against SC's onsite note using ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements (`standard_knowledge` when supplied, as untrusted reference data), the imported Excel checklist questions (`control.requirement`) as starting/baseline prompts rather than the ceiling, and practical ISO/IEC 27001 / ISO/IEC 27002 audit judgment for the clause and company context. Treat the note as SC's audit record; use only facts written in it. Do not map departments or invent customer scope, testing, observations, dates, causes, evidence or impacts. Uploaded files are archive-only; return evidence_ids: [].

- C (`met`, `comply`): the note establishes full conformity with applicable ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements and material checklist obligations.
- OFI (`met`, `ofi`): mandatory ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and checklist obligations are fully met and a specific optional practical enhancement (including ISO/IEC 27002 or operational improvement) exists. Never use OFI for a missing mandatory step.
- NC-Minor (`gap` or `partial`, `nc_minor`): a confirmed bounded lapse against a mandatory ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirement, checklist obligation, or stated procedure; recorded facts show no effect on ISMS capability. Never turn optional best practices into NCs.
- NC-Major (`gap` or `partial`, `nc_major`): a confirmed systemic breakdown or material effect on ISMS capability against a mandatory obligation. Do not infer severity from control importance.
- Needs Review: the note itself lacks facts needed to verify conformity against the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirement or material operational/checklist points, or lacks facts to decide gap scope or impact. Name the specific material missing fact directly, in one short sentence. An absent attachment is never a reason.

Use the exact validator-compatible fields: C/comply and OFI require `status=met`, an empty `requirement_gap`, and `system_impact=does_not_affect`; OFI also requires a concise nonempty recommendation. NC-Minor requires `status=gap` or `partial`, a nonempty `requirement_gap`, and `system_impact=does_not_affect` only when the note establishes no effect. NC-Major requires `status=gap` or `partial`, a nonempty `requirement_gap`, and `system_impact=affects` only when the note establishes material effect. For Needs Review use `system_impact=unknown`; preserve `status=gap` or `partial` and the requirement gap when nonfulfilment is confirmed but severity is uncertain. Use `status=needs_review` when implementation itself is uncertain. Never use unknown impact for C/OFI; if impact is unknown because the note lacks facts, use Needs Review.

Read all answers for the control together, including exceptions in supplemental notes, before choosing the grade. A positive opening sentence does not cancel a later confirmed gap. First identify the applicable ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and checklist obligations alongside practical operating reality (practice vs policy intent, scope, ownership, recency, exceptions) and the recorded facts; then distinguish mandatory fulfilment, optional enhancement, and nonfulfilment. Only confirmed nonfulfilment of a mandatory obligation receives an NC severity decision.

For OFI, explain why the mandatory process already works and which optional practical enhancement would help. Manual work, separate registers, or lack of automation are not gaps by themselves. A required review or mandatory standard/procedure step that was skipped is not OFI. Do not manufacture an improvement just to avoid C.

For NC-Minor, use the recorded extent and operating safeguards to assess capability. The auditor need not literally write “no system impact” when the stated facts establish that conclusion. A bounded lapse still requires a real unmet mandatory obligation; a small sample count alone does not prove low impact.

For NC-Major, a confirmed whole-process absence, failed recovery, or verified ineffective protection across the relevant scope can establish systemic impact without a completed security incident. Multiple lapses justify escalation only when the notes demonstrate a shared systemic failure. Never invent that linkage. If the facts cannot establish extent or capability, ask for the specific missing fact through Needs Review.

`control.grading_examples` contains up to three synthetic calibration cases selected for the current control. Use their distinctions only if their requirement_condition matches the applicable ISO/IEC 27001:2022 or checklist obligation. They are not customer notes, audit evidence, new obligations, a grade quota or permission to copy facts into the result. Assess the actual recorded answer; never borrow sample dates, counts, safeguards or incidents. These examples do not increase the number of assessment units.

Maintainer provenance and example scope are recorded in [references/knowledge-notes.md](references/knowledge-notes.md). Runtime grading examples are already supplied with the control; no additional file or tool lookup is needed during assessment.

Use `Document reference:` only for an actual document title recorded in the onsite note. A control ID/title, imported requirement, synthetic example or this knowledge source is not a customer document reference. Otherwise start directly with the recorded practice.

Do not default to C, require evidence files or force a grade distribution. Keep category and status consistent. Maintainers can consult [the workbook summary style reference](references/finding-summary-style.md); runtime writing guidance is included below.

SOURCE WORKBOOK AUDIT FINDING STYLE (takes precedence over generic summary/paragraph instructions): Write rationale in English like the original SC checklist findings, with direct auditor-record wording. Return one finding per control, combining related answers while retaining distinct material observations. Match the note structure to its contents: one simple sentence for a brief observation; short factual sentences for a process and its exception; topic-led lines for separate subjects. Use line breaks between distinct observations and, if supported, a blank line before the interview attribution. Do not force a single paragraph, a fixed sentence count or a word target. Use concrete operating subjects such as Access requests, Backup jobs, or Risk treatment plans; state current arrangements, actual counts/frequency/system/document references, work in progress and specific exceptions when recorded. Keep implemented work distinct from planned work. State a missing required step directly; preserve recorded scope and impact without guessing. A real named document may appear as Document reference: [actual title/code/version], and a recorded interview may end with Confirmed by interview with [actual recorded person/role]. Use these source-style lead-ins only when the answer supplies that source; do not start every finding with them. Never use a control title/ID as a document reference or invent interview sources, tests, dates or customer facts. Follow the workbook sentence rhythm and topic structure with readable English; do not imitate spelling errors. Avoid generic corporate prose, compliance verdicts, question-by-question retelling, duplicated facts, or routine introductory/outro text. A note such as "in place; owner assigned" does not justify expanding every question into a claim of implemented practice. Combine repeated ownership/review/implementation statements rather than giving each interview question its own sentence. Avoid long meta-commentary about generic phrases, placeholders, or what the answers fail to specify. When information is insufficient, state the actual recorded arrangement if any, followed by one short sentence naming the material fact not established. Do not quote placeholder answers or enumerate every missing subquestion. Do not demand a document name, sample size or named interviewee merely to improve writing. For example, when parties and requirements are not identified in the note, write "The recorded note does not identify the relevant interested parties or their information security requirements." Do not routinely prefix this with "Insufficient information in the recorded audit note:". The grade stays in its separate result cell; detailed grade reasoning belongs in audit.reason and follow-up in recommendation. Preserve a specific optional improvement in an OFI finding when recorded; never hide a confirmed gap for brevity. Needs Review identifies the exact missing fact. Fictional style-only example: note "change requests via MS Forms; no formal change procedure" becomes "IT change requests managed via MS Forms. No formal change procedure recorded." A note listing "internal: on-call capacity; external: cloud-provider dependency" may become "Internal issues: on-call capacity.
External issues: cloud-provider dependency." These demonstrate sentence/line structure only, not grades or customer facts. Do not copy facts, company names or observations from style examples.
