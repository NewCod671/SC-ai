# Original SC workbook finding style

Source: operator-supplied `6.1 SC202512-03-ECQSG-IAChecklist-V1.xlsx`, Clause 4-10 column AD and Annex 5-8 column AA. Examples were reviewed across both sheets, including brief scope and audit-cycle observations, topic-led internal/external issues, process exceptions, named document references and separate interview attributions. Source customer facts are not transferable to other assessments.

## Writing behavior

- Use direct English auditor-record sentences, retaining the source rhythm without reproducing spelling errors.
- A short observation may use one sentence. Multiple topics may use separate lines. Do not impose one paragraph, 2-4 sentences or a word target.
- Begin with the actual arrangement or recorded source. State missing work, plans in progress, specific exceptions and supported impact directly.
- Merge repeated answers but retain material systems, counts, frequencies and document details.
- Use `Document reference:` only for a real named document in the actual note. A control heading is not a document.
- If explicitly recorded, put `Confirmed by interview with ...` on a separate line, optionally after a blank line. Do not invent a person or role.
- Keep the grade, detailed grading explanation and follow-up in separate fields. Preserve optional improvements relevant to OFI and confirmed gaps relevant to NC.
- One combined finding per Control; no separate grading per subquestion.

## Fictional style illustrations

Input: `changes via MS Forms; no formal change procedure`.

> IT change requests managed via MS Forms. No formal change procedure recorded.

Input: `internal: on-call capacity; external: cloud-provider dependency`.

> Internal issues: on-call capacity.
> External issues: cloud-provider dependency.

These examples demonstrate wording and line structure only. They supply no default customer facts or audit grade.
